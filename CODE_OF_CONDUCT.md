# Code of conduct

We want participation in Rehearsal to be safe, respectful, and constructive.

Be considerate, assume good intent while addressing concrete impact, welcome questions,
and focus criticism on ideas and behavior. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
attention, doxxing, or publication of another person's private data are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to
protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports
will be handled with discretion; retaliation is prohibited.

By participating, you agree to follow this policy in repository spaces and when publicly
representing the project.
